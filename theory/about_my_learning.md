# My Learning Process and Study Methods

也许可以阐述一下自大学以来，我是如何学习的，以及我都学了些什么东西。以年和学期作为分段，我想是一种很好的选择。

*Let me elaborate on how I have been studying and what I have learned since I entered university. I think it is a good choice to break it down by academic year and semester.*

---

## Freshman
### Fall Semester

高考以后，我感到由衷的空虚，因而我觉得不得不找点事情做，所以开始的时候，我选择去看看书。于是乎我读了汉代那一部分的《资治通鉴》，以及一部名为《金山》的小说，这部小说是许多年前，讲述广东一家人远赴加拿大修筑铁路的故事，高度推荐。随后我又觉得无聊了，此时也伴随着录取结果出来，心情不好伴随着性格天生比较努力的缘故，以及我所具有的焦虑感，我开始看视频学习C，当然是极其基础的，但是这是一个起点，自此以后我不再阅读这些杂文小说，转而逐渐转为专业性的内容。

*After the Gaokao, I felt genuinely empty inside, so I felt like I had to find something to do. At first, I chose to read some books. I read the Han Dynasty part of the Zizhi Tongjian (a classic Chinese history book), and also a novel called Gold Mountain — it's about a family from Guangdong who traveled all the way to Canada to build the railway many years ago. Highly recommended. Then I got bored again, and around that time, my college admission results came out. Partly because I was feeling down, and partly because I'm just naturally the kind of person who works hard, as well as the anxiety that I carry with me, I started watching videos to learn C — of course, just the very basics. But that was a starting point. After that, I stopped reading those casual essays and novels, and shifted my focus to more professional and academic content.*

这一个学期，可讲的内容极少，只是跟随学校的课程按部就班的学习，刚上大学我也觉得陌生，没有投入太多学习的时间。当然，还有另外一个原因，此时我还认为学校的课程非常有用，足以涵盖我们的后续之用。

*There is relatively little to recount regarding this semester — I simply followed the university curriculum and studied in a routine manner. Having just entered college, I was still adjusting to the new environment, and thus I did not invest a significant amount of time in my academics. There was, of course, another contributing factor — at that point, I still held the belief that the university curriculum was highly valuable and would sufficiently equip us for our future needs.*

到这个学期结束，我决定不能仅仅满足于课业的基础，随后我在学校的图使馆借阅了一本关于`HTML`和`CSS`的书，以及一本关于`JavaScript`的书，我在家好好进行了阅读，此时我对于学习，主要停留在高中时关于“书”的一系列内容，所以我坚信书是学习的良好内容。

*Toward the end of that semester, I resolved that I could no longer confine myself to the foundational requirements of the coursework. Subsequently, I visited the university library and borrowed a book on HTML and CSS, as well as one on JavaScript, and devoted time to studying them thoroughly at home. At that juncture, my approach to learning remained largely shaped by my high school perspective — I regarded physical books as the primary vehicle of knowledge, and thus I firmly believed that reading books constituted an effective method of study.*

与此同时，第一个学期的分数和排名出来，我当时的目标就是考到差不多一半的位置就好，因为我已比较明确的不希望保研，所以我认为这对我没什么用处，但是作为一个完美主义者，我有时还是希望自己做到最好，同时我也总希望我最终的结果是超出我的目标的，而不是仅仅满足于我的目标，我记得我第一学期好像考了87名，也就是47.2%，这非常严重的激起了我的好胜心，我嘴上说没什么，可是我想下学期还是要在课业上做的更好些才行！

*In the meantime, my grades and class ranking for the first semester were released. My initial objective had been to place roughly in the middle of the class — around the 50th percentile — since I was already quite certain that I did not wish to pursue the graduate school recommendation pathway, and therefore I considered grades to be of little practical importance to me. However, being a perfectionist by nature, I occasionally still aspired to achieve my personal best. Moreover, I have always preferred my final outcomes to surpass my original targets, rather than merely meeting them. I recall that I ultimately ranked 87th, which corresponded to approximately the top 47.2%. This outcome significantly stirred my competitive instincts. Though I outwardly dismissed it as inconsequential, inwardly I resolved to perform more strongly in my coursework during the following semester.*
